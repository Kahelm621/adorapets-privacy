Adorapets Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Adorapets ("the app") is a puzzle game made by Khaotic Impulse Studios (Kristi Helm), an independent developer. This policy explains what information the app uses and why.

Short version

  • No accounts, no sign-in, and we never ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, or location.
  • Your game progress is saved only on your device.
  • The app shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID and related device/usage data to show and measure ads.

Information saved on your device

Your progress (levels, stars, coins, companions, settings such as sound) is stored locally on your device. It is not sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it. We have no server and cannot see or recover it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows optional rewarded ads (you choose to watch one for an in-game bonus) and banner ads on menu screens. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To serve, personalize (where permitted), measure ads, and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID),
  • IP address, general device and app information (model, OS version, app version, language), and
  • ad interaction data (for example, ad views and taps).

This data is collected and handled by Google, not by us. Learn more:

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Where required by law (for example, in the EEA, UK, or certain US states), the app asks for your consent through Google's consent message before personalized ads are shown.

Your choices: On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in Settings > Privacy > Ads (wording varies by device). Ads you see may then be less relevant.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not collect data for our own analytics, and we do not use any other third-party trackers besides Google AdMob.
  • There are no in-app chats, user profiles, or social features.

Children

Adorapets is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information through the app, contact us and we will help address it.

Data security and retention

Game progress stays on your device until you delete it. Data collected by Google AdMob is retained according to Google's policies.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date.
